# GEAR 🚗

**Smart Garage Management System**

GEAR is a modern garage management platform designed for automobile workshops and service centers. It helps workshop owners manage customers, vehicles, invoices, inventory, employees, and service history from a single application.

## ✨ Features

- 👥 Customer Management
- 🚘 Vehicle Management
- 🧾 GST & Non-GST Billing
- 📄 Invoice Generation
- 📦 Inventory & Spare Parts Management
- 💰 Due Payment Tracking
- 🔔 Service Reminders
- 👨‍🔧 Employee Management
- 📊 Reports & Analytics
- ☁️ Secure Cloud Backup
- 📱 Multi-Device Support
